Install on iPhone

Use Cole Fit like an app

After publishing this folder to a free HTTPS website, open its link in Safari, tap the Share icon and choose Add to Home Screen. Launch Cole Fit from the new Home Screen icon. Keep using the same website address so your saved history remains available.

Private history and sharing

Share the app, not your progress

Share only your website URL. Each person using their own phone/browser starts with an empty history and cannot see your saved workouts. Never share your exported history file; anyone with access to your unlocked phone and browser data may be able to view your entries.

Back up your training

Save each exercise using the green Save button. This app saves locally first. After cloud sign-in and setup it also syncs with your private account. Export a backup after workouts; data may be lost if Safari/site data is deleted, the app is removed, or storage is evicted. Opening in Safari and launching from your Home Screen may use different storage.
